About BANENYX

BANENYX is building an independent AI discovery platform to help people find useful tools for work, learning and business. Our goal is to make AI tools easier to discover and evaluate through clear categories and practical descriptions.

How listings are selected

Before publishing or recommending a tool, check what problem it solves, who it is useful for, its free or paid options, limitations, privacy considerations and official website. Tool descriptions on this starter site are brief summaries, not independent product reviews. Pricing is intentionally not stated here because it can change; check the provider's site.

BANENYX does not claim to have tested every listed product. Listings are not endorsements. Sponsored placements and affiliate relationships must be clearly labelled if introduced later.
